Sometimes the best opportunities are the ones that have been forgotten or poorly backfilled. By using the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine, you can look at a company’s "Meet the Team" page from a year ago and compare it to their current site. If you notice a specific role is missing now but hasn't been posted as an "open" role, there is a high chance that department is still feeling the gap of that departure, giving you a perfect opening to reach out.
